semantic anchor for phrases such as “第三条路径”. Recover its evidence chain and
source revision before expanding the explanation. Resolve personal sources through
`sources.personal_context_dir` and `sources.notes_roots`. Exact session/message
identifiers and private repository links remain in local evidence; public examples
use generic project names and safe public source references.

2. Connect inputs, relevant functions/thresholds, state transitions, and observable
   results. Separate implementation facts from explanatory inference.
3. Pin the revision used by the original card. Label later PRs, screenshots, and
   changed behavior with their own versions/dates.
4. Label each visual by its actual kind: gameplay screenshot, official map/locator
   raster, developer-tool screenshot, or mechanism schematic.
5. Redact private identifiers, usernames, service addresses, and sensitive paths
   while preserving evidence-bearing details. Cite the source and privacy crop.
6. Use screenshots for visible observations and source/log evidence for causes.
7. Check mobile legibility, clipping, overlap, labels, and source/version notes.

to the user's specific question and distinguish a system's implemented boundary
from a broader headline term.
